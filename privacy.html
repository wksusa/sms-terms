SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Program Name

Nova AI Assistant — WKS Restaurant Group

Introduction

This SMS Privacy Policy describes how WKS Restaurant Group ("WKS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our Nova AI Assistant SMS program. This policy applies specifically to information collected through our text messaging services.

Information We Collect

When you participate in our SMS program, we may collect the following information:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Records of your opt-in consent (including timestamp and method of consent)
  • Message content exchanged with the Nova AI Assistant
  • Information related to your service requests, such as work order details and equipment issue descriptions

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our SMS program solely to operate and deliver the Nova AI Assistant service, including:

  • Providing facilities support and troubleshooting guidance
  • Creating and updating maintenance work orders
  • Sending work order status updates and technician notifications
  • Responding to your service inquiries
  • Maintaining consent records for compliance purposes

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or distribute any text messaging opt-in data or consent information with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile information, including your phone number and opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional use. This applies to all categories of data collected through our SMS program.

Data Protection

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your mobile information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain your mobile phone number and consent records for as long as you remain opted in to our SMS program and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal and regulatory obligations. Message content may be retained for the duration necessary to resolve your service requests.

Your Choices

You may opt out of our SMS program at any time by texting STOP to the number from which you received messages. Upon opting out, we will cease sending messages and retain only the records required for compliance purposes.
